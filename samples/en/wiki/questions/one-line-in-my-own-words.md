# One line in my own words is what makes a note mine

## Insight

Notes with a single sentence I wrote myself are remembered better than notes I only read, even when the sentence is short and obvious.

## Why it may matter

If true, the cheapest way to keep a note is to add one line at the bottom when I first read it: what it means for me or where I would use it. Nothing else about the note has to change.

## Evidence

- On day five of [a week of recalling notes](../../sources/a-week-of-recalling-notes.md), the notes with my own line came back far better than the ones without.
