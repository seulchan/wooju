# Does rereading help at all?

## Question

Rereading a note feels productive. Does it do anything for memory, compared with trying to recall the note first?

## What we know

- A [week of recalling notes before rereading them](../../sources/a-week-of-recalling-notes.md) left the recalled notes in memory and the merely reread ones feeling unread.
- The [forgetting curve](../../sources/forgetting-curve.md) flattens after a review, but it does not say whether the review must be a recall attempt or can be a passive reread.

## What we don't know

- Whether a reread right after a failed recall is what makes the recall useful, or whether recall alone does it.
- Whether this holds for long, complex notes as well as short factual ones.

## Current answer

Not settled. Rereading alone seems to add little, but a reread after a recall attempt looks useful. Needs more than one week of notes to say.
