# Spacing effect

## What it is

Reviews spread out over time produce longer-lasting memory than the same reviews packed into one session. The effect shows up for facts, words, and skills, and in children and adults alike.

## Why it matters

It explains why cramming works for tomorrow's exam and fails for next month, and it is the reason a note is worth a second look a week later rather than a second look right away. The [forgetting curve](../../sources/forgetting-curve.md) shows when memory drops; spacing puts the review just before the drop.

## Example

Reading a note three times on Monday leaves little by Friday. Reading it on Monday, Thursday and the following Monday leaves most of it a month later. [Spaced repetition](../../sources/spaced-repetition.md) turns this into a schedule.
