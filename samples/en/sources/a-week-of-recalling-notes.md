# A week of recalling notes before rereading them

## One-line Summary

Trying to recall a note before opening it was hard on the first day and easier each day after; notes with a line in my own words stuck best.

## Key Ideas

- On the first try I recalled only the topic, never the numbers.
- By the second or third attempt most of the note came back.
- Notes I had written one sentence about were remembered far better than notes I had only read.
- Notes I never tried to recall felt as if I had never read them.

## Limitations / Caveats

- One person, one week, two notes: a diary, not a study.
- I chose which notes to recall, so the easier ones may have been picked.
