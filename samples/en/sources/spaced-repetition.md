# Spaced repetition

## One-line Summary

Reviewing material at growing intervals keeps it in memory with far fewer repetitions than cramming.

## Key Ideas

- Each successful review is followed by a longer gap before the next one; a failed review shortens the gap again.
- The method rests on the spacing effect: the same number of reviews spread over time beats the same number in one sitting.
- Flashcard programs such as SuperMemo and Anki schedule the gaps automatically.
- It works best for material you can test yourself on, such as facts, vocabulary and definitions.

## Limitations / Caveats

- Most studies measure recall of discrete facts; evidence for complex skills and understanding is thinner.
- Scheduling only helps if the reviews actually happen.
